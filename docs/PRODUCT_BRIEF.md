# Product brief

## Vision
Turn complex statistical reports into queryable, explorable and verifiable knowledge.

## Problem
Important information in long reports is distributed across narrative text, tables, charts, appendices and footnotes. Users need accurate answers and a way to verify their sources.

## Core experience
A user uploads a report, asks a natural-language question, receives a concise answer with citations, and opens the exact supporting page and highlighted evidence. Comparisons and calculations expose their source values and method.

## Capabilities
- Process text-based and scanned PDFs.
- Detect headings, paragraphs, tables, charts, captions and footnotes.
- Preserve PDF page index separately from printed page labels.
- Retrieve narrative and structured table evidence.
- Handle lookup, comparison, aggregation, trend and multi-hop questions.
- Calculate with deterministic code.
- Cite supporting evidence and abstain when unsupported.

## Non-goals
- Claiming exact values from charts when only estimates are recoverable.
- Inventing facts absent from the uploaded reports.
- Silently reconciling incompatible definitions.
- Claiming universal compatibility with every PDF layout.

## Quality bar
Every answer should be traceable to the correct document, page, entity, metric, period and unit. If these cannot be established, expose the uncertainty.
